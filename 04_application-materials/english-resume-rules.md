# English Resume Rules / 英文简历规则

1. Use only confirmed facts from `01_profile/` and a real job description. Do not invent roles, metrics, skills, or outcomes.
2. Default order for a new graduate: Contact, Education, Experience, Projects, Skills. Rebalance Experience and Projects for the role.
3. Write each bullet as **action + task or method + verifiable result**. Use concise, natural English; do not translate Chinese bullets word for word.
4. Keep the most relevant 2–4 bullets per entry. Shorten or remove weakly relevant material before shrinking the font.
5. Use one date style throughout, such as `Sep 2025 – Dec 2025`.
6. Include numbers only when the underlying evidence is available. Use `about` or `approximately` for supported estimates. Do not turn team outcomes into solo ownership.
7. Do not include a photo, age, gender, marital status, identity number, or unconfirmed links.
8. Use `templates/resume-en.html`. Preserve the single-column A4 layout and selectable text. Check print preview, links, spelling, grammar, and page count.
9. Deliver as a draft for the applicant's review. Do not submit an application.
